# Visual Identity

## Concept

The identity shows source folders moving through a validation gate into an organized modular packet. The cover expands that metaphor into the repository's real flow: source records, folder-scoped review, feedback decisions, and a reusable candidate bundle.

## Palette

- Forest: `#153F35`
- Teal: `#168C88`
- Gold: `#E7B54A`
- Coral: `#D96C4F`
- Paper: `#F4F0E6`
- Charcoal: `#182321`

Use forest and teal for structure, gold for promoted or selected information, coral for review attention, and paper for readable backgrounds.

## Assets

All reusable assets live in [`docs/assets/brand/`](assets/brand/). The PNG logo variants are ready for repository and social use. `logo-mask.svg` is the editable single-color mask. `manifest.json` records dimensions, generation settings, and hashes.

Keep the full mark visible with clear space around it. Do not stretch it, crop the folders or packet, add unsupported text inside it, or use the source chroma background as a final asset.

## Accessibility

Use descriptive alt text such as: "Source folders pass through validation into an organized domain-kit packet." Do not rely on green checks or coral crosses alone; pair color with labels in surrounding content.
